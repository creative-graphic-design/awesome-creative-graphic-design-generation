# Figure sources

`overview.tex`, `edit_protocol.tex`, and `research_map.tex` are self-contained standalone TikZ figures. Their manuscript float wrappers (`*_figure.tex`) store captions and labels, and include the generated PDFs in `../pdf/`. Run `make figures` to render all three vector PDFs.

The research map's 67 labels are linked to their cited bibliography entries in `research_map_sources.csv`. `paper/scripts/check_manuscript.py` checks this correspondence and each citation year. See `paper/RESEARCH_MAP_AUDIT.md` for selection, known preprint-versus-venue year differences, and limits of evidence.

`research_map_narrative.tex` is the audited input registry for a second original, **26-study illustrated map** with task lanes, technique-colored borders and conceptual output sketches. Its exact nodes and citation years are checked against `research_map_narrative_sources.csv` by `check_manuscript.py`. The 67-node `research_map.tex` remains the complete, separately generated atlas; the narrative diagram does not replace the original audited crosswalk. `paper/scripts/render_research_map_concept.py` reads the same 26-node TeX declarations and CSV audit, embeds source-prepared vector institutional symbols for 21 cards, and generates `figs/pdf/research_map_concept.svg` and `.pdf`. `paper/scripts/check_research_map_concept.py` validates the resulting SVG and unique logo references.

An experimental, **not manuscript-selected**, 67-study research landscape can be generated by `paper/scripts/render_research_landscape.py` from the already audited `research_map_sources.csv`. Layout dimensions and colors are centralized tokens; do not set per-paper coordinates. The experimental SVG/PDF is built manually, not in the standard figure build. The manuscript retains `research_map.tex` until a new design passes scientific and visual review. The separate 26-study logo-bearing narrative figure remains unchanged.
