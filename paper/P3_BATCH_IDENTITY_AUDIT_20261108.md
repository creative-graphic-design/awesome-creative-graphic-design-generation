## Source and counting policy

- Names and title variants were reconciled by publisher DOI/arXiv identifier, not approximate title matching. CGD-005 has a singular/plural catalogue-title variant; CGD-017, CGD-037 and CGD-053 are method short names with longer canonical paper titles.
- `abstract_index` means a primary author/publisher abstract or bibliographic description was examined, **not** the original experimental tables, implementation, or supplements. `catalogue_only` for CGD-013, CGD-167 and CGD-040 means bibliographic metadata was checked but the original experimental evidence was not obtained.
- Citation matching is not a systematic selection decision: all 26 remain `editorial_decision=pending`, and no automatic exclusion was recorded.
- Exact authors, formal venue year and revisions should be rechecked before submission, especially 2024-online/2025-volume journal entries and the early/short versions of UIST papers.
- The full list requires manual scientific and experimental-source audit, final PDF/typeset review, and eventual author approval.
