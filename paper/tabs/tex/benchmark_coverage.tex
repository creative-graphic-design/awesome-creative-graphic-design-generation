\ac{bizgeneval} & Rendered commercial visuals & Layout, typography, attributes, knowledge checklists & Successful raster rendering does not guarantee editable source objects.\\
\ac{aesevalbench} & Graphic-design aesthetic assessment & Aesthetic judgment, region selection, localization & Judge performance is not design editing performance.\\
\ac{designprobe} & Design understanding & Attribute recognition, style and semantic reasoning & Understanding is not execution of a design modification.\\

\ac{pptarena} & Editable presentation decks & Real-slide edits, structural and visual validation & Native slide editing is not equivalent to static single-slide rendering.\\
\ac{sciflowbench} & Scientific diagram images & Diagram connectivity via inverse parsing & Structural graph validity and raster similarity are different dimensions.\\
